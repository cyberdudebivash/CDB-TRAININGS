Gate A never reads a status that someone typed in to decide completeness. A module is `IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED` when its files exist, `TECHNICALLY_VERIFIED` only when the recorded execution evidence passed on at least three interpreters, `REVIEW_REQUIRED` only when an adversarial review is recorded, and a course is `TRAINING_CONTENT_GO` only when every mandatory criterion is MET, the quality score is at least 95 and named humans have approved it. The score is necessary but never sufficient.
