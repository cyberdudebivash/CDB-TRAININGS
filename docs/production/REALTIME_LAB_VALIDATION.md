## 3. Rules the labs follow

- A timing claim in a lesson is a measured ratio or bound asserted by an executed test (for example the concurrency speed-up is measured against sequential time on the same fixture), not a constant copied from a source.
- Every lab has a local, cost-free alternative: the loopback `ScriptedServer` and the seeded generators need no network, account or paid service.
- No lab calls a paid service. Any use of a paid or external service requires the owner's explicit approval first.
