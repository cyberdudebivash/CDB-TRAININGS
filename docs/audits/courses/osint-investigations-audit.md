### F-OSINT-002 (P0) Proposes redistributing third-party tools under a 'CyberDudeBivash proprietary license'

- **p. 7:** 'Branded & Secure -> Licensed under the CyberDudeBivash proprietary license'.
- **p. 14:** 'distributed under a CyberDudeBivash proprietary license ... Verified Integrity'.
- **p. 6:** Preinstalled tools named: theHarvester, Recon-ng, Sherlock, Maltego CE, SpiderFoot HX, ExifTool.

theHarvester is GPL-2.0-only, recon-ng is GPL-3.0 and Sherlock is MIT (repository pages). Copyleft components cannot be relicensed as proprietary, and Maltego CE / SpiderFoot HX terms were not checked (NEEDS_VERIFICATION). This is a licensing/legal blocker for any VM or bundle distribution. Not legal advice; counsel review required.

**Verification:** PRIMARY_SOURCE. **Recommendation:** Do not ship a bundled VM until each component's licence and redistribution terms are reviewed; prefer install scripts that fetch tools from upstream.
**Remediation:** NOT_STARTED - docs/audits/IP_AND_LICENSE_REVIEW.md (open action).
