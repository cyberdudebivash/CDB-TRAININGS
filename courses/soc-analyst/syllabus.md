# Syllabus - SOC Analyst Professional Pathway

Planning estimates, not measured learner times. Levels: 1 core practitioner, 2 independent professional, 3 advanced specialist.
Prerequisite: COURSE-FND-DIGITAL (Level 0 foundations) or demonstrated equivalent. Source of truth: `catalog/courses.yaml`.

| Module | Title | Level | Hours | Primary outcome | State |
|---|---|:-:|--:|---|---|
| SOC-M01 | SOC fundamentals, roles and the alert lifecycle | 1 | 4 | Explain the alert lifecycle and map each stage to its role and artefact | Planned |
| SOC-M02 | Telemetry: Windows, Linux and network logs | 1 | 8 | Identify the log source and event ID or field that evidences a behaviour | Planned |
| SOC-M03 | Log parsing, normalisation and query languages | 1 | 8 | Parse a raw log sample and answer five investigative questions with queries | Planned |
| **SOC-M04** | **Authentication-attack detection and triage** | 2 | 6 | Build and validate brute-force and spray detections and triage the alerts | **Built (pilot)** |
| SOC-M05 | Alert triage and case management | 2 | 6 | Triage ten synthetic alerts and produce a handover record | Planned |
| SOC-M06 | Detection engineering with Sigma and ATT&CK | 2 | 10 | Author a Sigma rule and show its precision and recall | Planned |
| SOC-M07 | Threat-intelligence enrichment and false-positive reduction | 2 | 6 | Cut a noisy rule's false positives by 50% without losing a true positive | Planned |
| SOC-M08 | Incident investigation and evidence handling | 2 | 8 | Reconstruct a timeline and map actions to CSF 2.0 Functions | Planned |
| SOC-M09 | Threat hunting | 3 | 8 | Plan and run a hypothesis-driven hunt | Planned |
| SOC-M10 | Malware-analysis fundamentals (isolated lab) | 3 | 8 | Statically triage a benign sample and document indicators | Planned |
| SOC-M11 | Executive and technical incident reporting | 3 | 4 | Write an executive summary and technical appendix that pass the rubric | Planned |
| SOC-M12 | Capstone: end-to-end SOC investigation | 3 | 12 | Investigate a multi-stage synthetic intrusion and improve one detection | Planned |

**Competency vs completion.** Module completion = all lab checks and the rubric pass rule met. Verified skill mastery needs the capstone. Professional experience cannot be
certified by this course.
